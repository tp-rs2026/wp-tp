About Tapara

Better economics at every level.

Tapara is a commerce platform that makes every transaction — from payment processing to supplier procurement — more efficient for independent businesses and their customers.

format_quote

Independent businesses pay too much at every level of the stack. Processing fees take 2–3% of every sale. Platform subscriptions cost hundreds a month. And when it comes to sourcing products, they pay retail prices because they buy alone.

Tapara fixes this layer by layer. Open banking cuts processing fees. A single modular platform replaces five paid tools. And as merchants join, their collective purchasing power unlocks supplier pricing that no single shop could negotiate alone.

Our principles

What we believe

location_on

Localise the transaction

Every dollar that doesn't leave the community through card networks, platform fees, or middlemen is a dollar that stays between the merchant and their customer.

groups

Economics of scale, without the scale

One cafe can't negotiate wholesale pricing. A thousand cafes on one platform can. Tapara pools purchasing power so independents access the economics that only chains get today.

hub

One platform, compounding value

Payments, loyalty, website, procurement — each app makes the others more valuable. The more merchants join, the better the economics get for everyone.
